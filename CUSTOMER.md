# Customer Overview

## What Is This?

**Love Experience** is a web app that lets you create an unforgettable, cinematic Valentine's Day or proposal experience for someone special — and share it via a single link.

Instead of sending a card or a text, you build an entire personalized world: their name appears in the stars, they play through romantic mini-games, and the experience builds up to a heartfelt message and a question — all wrapped in Apple-level polish and Disney-level magic.

## How It Works

### 1. You Build (The Sender)

Open the **Builder Dashboard** and configure everything:

- **Two names** (yours and theirs) — the only required fields
- **World theme** — Aurora Night, Deep Galaxy, Cherry Blossom, Golden Sunset, Magic Forest, Deep Ocean, or Black & Gold
- **Atmosphere** — weather, particle intensity
- **Your message** — a quote, a heartfelt letter, pet names, a date suggestion
- **AI-generated poem** — click a button and AI writes a personalized love poem for them
- **Mini-games** — choose which of 10 games they'll play (Heart Catch, Memory Match, Cupid's Arrow, and more)
- **Photos & voice notes** — add personal photos and record a voice message
- **Music** — link a Spotify playlist or YouTube song
- **Secrets** — hide a secret code that reveals a hidden message
- **Ending style** — fireworks, floating lanterns, constellations, or sunrise

When you're done, click **"Generate unique link"**. You get a shareable URL like:
```
https://your-site.com/#/story/ABC12345
```

### 2. They Experience (The Receiver)

They open the link. **Zero inputs required** — everything is already configured.

The experience begins immediately:

1. **Cinematic opening** — Black screen, a heartbeat, "Searching the universe... Scanning memories... Locating one special person..." → "Receiver located: {their name}" → "Welcome, {their name}. Someone spent an unreasonable amount of time creating this world just for you."

2. **The journey** — A series of mini-games, each unlocking surprises:
   - Catch falling hearts
   - Match pairs from memory
   - Find the hidden heart
   - Whack popping hearts
   - Shoot Cupid's arrows
   - Spin the wheel of love
   - Pop floating bubble hearts
   - Dig for buried treasure
   - Test your reflexes
   - Build a bouquet of flowers

   Their name appears magically throughout — as glowing stars, in the constellations, whispered by NPCs.

3. **The question** — "Will you be my Valentine?" with a glowing YES button (that slowly grows) and a mischievous NO button that never actually says no (it turns into a potato, a cat, a cloud, then dissolves into confetti).

4. **The finale** — Fireworks, their name written in the stars as a constellation, your handwritten message revealed, photos displayed as polaroids, the AI poem, your voice note — ending with a glowing button: **"Let's Make This Date Happen"**.

### 3. You Know (The Sender)

Back in the Builder Dashboard, you see a live **"Receiver activity"** panel:

- ✅ Whether they opened the link
- 🗺️ How far they got (opening → journey → question → finale)
- 💞 Whether they pressed YES
- 💖 **Whether they accepted the date** (when they click the final button)

You'll know the moment they say yes.

## Who Is It For?

- **Couples** celebrating Valentine's Day, anniversaries, or proposals
- **Long-distance relationships** who want to share something special
- **Anyone** who wants to make someone feel like a whole world was built just for them

## Privacy

- No accounts, no login
- Stories are stored in a database (for link sharing)
- Photos and voice notes stay in the browser (IndexedDB) — never uploaded
- The sender sees activity stats (opened, progress, accepted) but no personal data
- No analytics, no tracking, no ads

## Pricing

The app is free to run locally. For public deployment, hosting costs apply (Vercel free tier is sufficient for personal use).
